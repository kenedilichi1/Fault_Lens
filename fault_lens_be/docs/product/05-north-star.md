# FaultLens North Star

## Why FaultLens Exists

Modern engineering teams do not suffer from a lack of observability.

They suffer from a lack of understanding.

Every production incident generates thousands of logs, hundreds of metrics, dozens of alerts, and countless dashboards.

Yet engineers still spend hours asking the same questions.

What happened?

Why did it happen?

Where should I start?

FaultLens exists to answer those questions.

---

# The Problem

Observability tools are excellent at collecting data.

They are much less effective at helping engineers understand that data.

During an incident, engineers waste valuable time:

- Searching through logs
- Comparing dashboards
- Checking deployments
- Looking through previous incidents
- Asking teammates
- Guessing possible causes

The problem is not data.

The problem is context.

---

# Our Mission

Help engineering teams resolve production incidents faster by transforming operational data into actionable intelligence.

---

# Our Vision

A future where every production incident comes with:

- An explanation
- A probable root cause
- A timeline of events
- Similar historical incidents
- Suggested remediation steps

Before an engineer asks for them.

# Product Decision Framework

Every proposed feature should answer these questions.

1. Does it help engineers understand production problems?

2. Does it reduce Mean Time To Resolution?

3. Does it reduce cognitive load during incidents?

4. Does it strengthen AI-powered incident intelligence?

5. Would users miss it during an outage?

If the answer to most of these questions is "no", the feature probably does not belong in FaultLens.

---

# The North Star

When engineers receive an alert, they should not have to investigate from scratch.

FaultLens should already have done most of the thinking.

The engineer's role shifts from searching for information to validating recommendations and making informed decisions.

That is the future we are building.
